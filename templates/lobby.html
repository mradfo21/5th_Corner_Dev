// what you're stepping into

A machine that dreams a nightmare, one turn at a time.

SOMEWHERE isn't a scripted game — it's a story engine. It writes the scene, paints it, and reacts to you, live. Here's what makes each run yours alone.

▚

Scenes drawn live

Every frame is generated the instant you reach it — a first-person camcorder feed into a world that has never existed before.

⌥

Choices that fork the world

Four options every turn, or type your own. The story bends around what you do — not a branch you picked from a script.

✝

Permanent death

One life. When the signal cuts, that run is over — and it's sealed to a VHS death-tape you can play back.

◔

The clock is always running

Fifteen seconds to act. Hesitate and the world moves without you — and the world is rarely kind.

❖

A world that remembers

Location, injuries, inventory, the things you've seen — all tracked and folded back into every scene that follows.

⟳

Pick up where you left off

Every run gets a private link. Bookmark it, share the code, and slip back into your instance from any device.

// the loop

See it. Read it. Decide. Live with it.

One turn, on repeat, until the signal cuts.

  1. 01

    The scene loads

    A live-drawn frame drops you exactly where you are — a highway, a doorway, the dark between the trees.

  2. 02

    The dispatch reads

    A terse, present-tense read of what's happening around you — grounded in what the camera can actually see.

  3. 03

    You choose

    Four moves, or your own words. Fifteen seconds on the clock. No take-backs.

  4. 04

    The world answers

    Consequences resolve, the world evolves, a new frame is drawn — and the loop begins again.

  4. Survive as long as you can. Your run saves itself. Death is permanent, so make it count.
