how it plays

See a place. Pick a move. Live with it.

01

New scenes each turn

Images and story are generated as you go. You don't walk a pre-written path — the place forms around what you do.

02

Fifteen seconds to decide

Four options, or type something yourself. Wait too long and the game acts without you.

03

One life

No checkpoints. No continue from death. When a run ends, it's done — and you can watch the tape after.

04

State carries forward

Injuries, inventory, people, and places you leave behind stay in the save. Coming back later picks up mid-run.

How does this work?
  1. Start a run. You'll get a private link you can bookmark.
  2. Look at the scene, read the dispatch, decide what to do.
  3. Choose one of four options, or type your own. You have fifteen seconds.
  4. Progress saves automatically. If you die, that run is over.
